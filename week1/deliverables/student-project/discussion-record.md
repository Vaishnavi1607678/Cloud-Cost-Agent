## X comment checklist (Section 6). Every comment must:

1. Refer to a **specific item** in the original post
2. Say what you **understand**
3. Say what you **don't understand**
4. Give **one result** from your work, when relevant
5. Ask **one clear question**

Never write "Great insight" or comments with no technical content. Be honest about your level.

**Example shape** (write your own words, matched to the real post):
> You mention [specific item from their post]. I understand that [your understanding]. In my small simulation, [one result, e.g. "a cost-aware agent missed 0.1 compromises per 40 cases vs 2.5 for a threshold alert, but raised more false alarms"]. What I don't understand is [gap]. [One question]?

---

## After each discussion (Section 5)

Paste the full discussion into an AI assistant with this prompt, then **check the summary against the original thread** before using it:

```text
Here is a discussion about my cloud cost agent (problem: ignore/investigate/limit/report an
AWS cost spike when the cause is unknown). Using only what people actually wrote:
1. Make a short summary of the discussion.
2. Identify each point of disagreement.
3. Identify a new testable assumption.
4. Identify a change for the agent.
5. Identify information that I must verify.
Quote the person when you attribute a claim. Do not add facts that are not in the thread.
```
